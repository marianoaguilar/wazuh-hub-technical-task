## Summary
PeerSpot lists 51 Wazuh reviews with an average of 3.7/5. Reviewers like the price (free core), file
integrity monitoring, compliance and MITRE mapping, EDR-like features and, often, the documentation.
Dislikes include the lack of AI/ML features, storage and stability problems at scale, support
depending on community channels, agent load on weak laptops, and some configuration effort.
G2, Capterra and Gartner Peer Insights could not be fetched.
